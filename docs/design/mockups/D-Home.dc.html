How I think
& build

The same three turns of the drum on every project, big or small. Good products start with good questions.

T 1/3

Think

Understand the problem, ask the questions and choose priorities. Who is this for, and what happens if we don't build it?

M M 2/3

Make

Turn the intention into a working product: UX, implementation, collaboration and running it in production.

R 3/3

Rethink

Validate, learn and change direction. Feedback, iteration, and the things I chose not to build.
