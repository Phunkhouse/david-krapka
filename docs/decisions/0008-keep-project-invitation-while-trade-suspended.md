# 0008. Keep the project invitation while the trade is suspended

Date: 2026-11-04  
Status: accepted

## Context

David's trade licence (IČO) is suspended while he is on social-security support, and will be reactivated after he finds a job. The site is a showcase and job search, but the approved design also invites potential clients ("Have a project? → Start a project"). The [legal planning audit](../audits/2026-11-04/legal-planning.md) flagged this as LEG-001: whether publicly inviting projects during a suspension counts as operating the trade is a legal-classification question the audit could not settle, and the conditions of the social-security support were outside its scope.

## Decision

- Keep the invitation as designed. It is an invitation to talk, delivered as an email link. David accepts no paid work while the trade is suspended.
- Do not publish prices, rates, terms or availability claims for paid services while the trade is suspended.
- Business identification (name, place of business, IČO; LEG-002) is added when David accepts paid work or the IČO is reactivated, whichever comes first. The footer reserves a slot for it from milestone 2.

## Alternatives considered

- **Remove or reword the invitation until reactivation** (the audit's proposal). Lowest legal risk, but David judged the invitation harmless: it only starts a conversation.
- **Ask the trade licensing office or the office paying the support first.** Would settle the question, not done now.

## Consequences

- The question stays unresolved, so this is an accepted risk owned by David, not a legal conclusion. The audit does not clear it.
- The Pre-launch legal audit in milestone 6 reviews the rendered wording again.
- Revisit before the first paid job, when the IČO is reactivated, if the site starts to describe services in more detail, or if the office paying the support sets conditions on self-employment or advertising.
