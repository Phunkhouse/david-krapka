## Summary

UpVideo's company site presents its corporate video production (conference videos, success stories, image videos and podcasts) for clients such as SAP and EY and leads visitors to a contact form. The first version went live in 2016. In March 2025 it got its third redesign and moved to Next.js.

## What I owned

The design and the build, from the first version on. The logo is UpVideo's own, from 2016. The earlier versions were mostly plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript; the March 2025 redesign moved the site to Next.js.

## What I would <Re>re</Re>think

If I built it today, I would choose Astro. A marketing site that ends in a contact form needs little JavaScript, and Astro would ship less of it, so the pages would load faster.
