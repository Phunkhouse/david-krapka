## Summary

Vladan Vaněk is a communication consultant: strategy, content and ghostwriting, media relations and training. His site presents those services and his experience, with courses, a blog and references, and ends in a contact form. It went live in July 2025.

## What I owned

The design, including the logo, and the build. Like Eucal it runs on Next.js with its content in Sanity. GSAP animations and an Embla carousel give the pages some movement without getting in the way of the content.

The design also became the basis of his brand. He took its colours, logo and fonts into his own posts on social media.

## What I would <Re>re</Re>think

Today I would pick Astro and Keystatic. Astro ships little JavaScript, so the pages would load faster. Vladan knows GitHub, so Keystatic would keep his posts as files in the site's repository, with a simple editor on top: no external service or account to maintain, and every change versioned in git.
